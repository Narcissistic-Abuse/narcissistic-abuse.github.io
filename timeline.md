# Timeline

This section will outline key events in chronological order.  
You can build it up as you go — start simple, just a few dates and short notes.

**Example:**

- **August 2023** — First contact and early conversations.  
- **January 2024** — First major conflict and emotional withdrawal.  
- **June 2024** — False accusations and online sabotage begin.  
- **December 2024** — Police involvement and charges filed.  
- **2025 onward** — Investigation, evidence gathering, and recovery.

Add or expand this list anytime. Each update automatically appears when you commit.
